HOW IT WORKS

A simple journey from crop to payment.

Fasal Setu sits around procurement operations and makes the farmer-facing journey understandable and predictable.

1

Register with OTP

Only farmers self-register. Phone or email is verified before the account is created.

2

Tell us your crop

Save crop, season, land and expected quantity information to support recommendations.

3

Find a better centre

Compare eligible centres by location, queue, capacity and available slots.

4

Book and check in

Book a slot and use the digital gate pass/QR at the procurement centre.

5

Track the queue

See farmers ahead and the estimated wait instead of standing without information.

6

Track procurement and payment

Follow quality, weighment, procurement, bill and payment stages in one timeline.
